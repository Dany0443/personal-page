What I'm interested in

A quick look at the areas in tech that I spend the most time exploring and learning about.

Open Source Software

I'm a big advocate for open source. Whenever a viable open-source alternative exists, I will always choose it over proprietary software. I value privacy, transparency, having actual ownership over my tools, and knowing what is running on my hardware.

Servers & Infrastructure

I really enjoy working with servers and self-hosting services for my own daily needs. Managing homelab machines, spinning up containers, and tweaking networking and infrastructure as a whole is where I spend most of my time tinkering.

Always Learning

I'm still learning a ton along the way. Most of what I know comes from hands-on experimentation - trying out new setups, troubleshooting when things break, and figuring out how systems fit together under the hood.
